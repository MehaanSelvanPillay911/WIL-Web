About Us

Our Purpose

Escape Rooms SA is an informational website created to introduce people to the concept of escape rooms and highlight different escape room experiences available in KwaZulu-Natal.

The website is not about a specific escape room experience. Instead, it provides information about escape rooms and helps visitors discover different experiences.

What Is An Escape Room?

An escape room is an interactive game where a group of people enters a themed room and works together to solve puzzles, discover clues and complete challenges.

Players usually have a limited amount of time to complete their objectives. Communication, teamwork, observation and problem-solving are important parts of the experience.

Why Do People Enjoy Escape Rooms?

  • They encourage teamwork.
  • They involve solving puzzles and mysteries.
  • They provide an interactive experience.
  • They can be enjoyed with friends and family.
  • Different rooms offer different themes and challenges.
