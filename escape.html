Escape Rooms in KwaZulu-Natal

Discover different escape room experiences available throughout KwaZulu-Natal.

Mindgame Escape - Umhlanga

Mindgame Escape offers themed escape room experiences where players work together to find clues, solve puzzles and complete their mission before the time runs out.

The Order of the 9

Players investigate a mysterious secret society while searching for clues and solving puzzles.

The Forgotten Temple

Players explore an ancient Mayan temple while solving puzzles and trying to escape.

Bunker 51

Bunker 51 is a themed escape room experience involving a mysterious unidentified flying object. Players must investigate the mystery and complete their mission.

Lost In Time - Mystery Cabin

Mystery Cabin is an escape room experience in Kloof. Players investigate a strange cabin while searching for clues and solving puzzles.

Lost In Time - The Abandoned Reactor

The Abandoned Reactor is another escape room experience at Lost In Time. Players take part in a mission involving Nikola Tesla's research and a hidden reactor.

CryptIQ Escape Games

CryptIQ Escape Games is located in the Midlands Meander. The venue offers escape room experiences and other activities such as virtual reality.

How Escape Rooms Work

  1. Players enter a themed room.
  2. The story and objectives are explained.
  3. Players search for clues.
  4. Players solve puzzles and challenges.
  5. The team works together to complete the objective.
  6. Players try to finish before the time runs out.
